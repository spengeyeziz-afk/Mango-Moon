### 🏝️ About the Game

Welcome to **Mango Moon**.

A dirt path, a pink cottage, and a wooden sign. Beyond them is a Jamaican cove where mango, cherry, and tamarind ripen in the sun. This is a slow farming game about growing fruit, cooking it, and waiting for the good bottles.

Start on **Marva’s Cove**. Plant the orchard, pick what is ready, and carry cherry and tamarind to Granny Marva. She sets jam the same morning and leaves wine underground for months and years.

When the cove is working, take the boat to **Bonnet Cay**. Raise chickens, collect eggs, go fishing, and cook beach jerk for the crowd.
